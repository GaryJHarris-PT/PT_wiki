SAHI's Full Positional Relation Family

For quick reference, here are all of SAHI's built-in positional relation operators:

- **_above(anchor)** — Visually above the anchor element
- **_under(anchor)** — Visually below the anchor element
- **_rightOf(anchor)** — Visually to the right of the anchor element
- **_leftOf(anchor)** — Visually to the left of the anchor element
- **_near(anchor)** — In close visual proximity to the anchor element
- **_in(parent)** — Nested inside a specific DOM container/parent element
